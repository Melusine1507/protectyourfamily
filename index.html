East Coast Gas Stations Run Dry

Portable power stations, rechargeable lighting, and passive cooling and heating techniques that require no electricity and no fuel.

February 2021 — Oldsmar, Florida
Water Treatment Plant Attacked
An attacker remotely attempted to increase lye in the water supply to 111 times the safe level. One alert operator stopped it. 15,000 people nearly poisoned.
Your Preparation
Water Security
Gravity-fed home filtration, a stored water reserve, and conservation techniques that stretch every drop across days of disruption.
The Threat
October 2016 — Dyn DNS Attack
East Coast Internet Goes Dark
Three college students used a botnet of home devices to take down Twitter, Netflix, CNN, and thousands of sites for most of a day.
Your Preparation
Communications Backup
A hand-crank NOAA emergency radio and a written family communication plan that works when every digital channel is offline.
The Threat
2012 — Operation Ababil
46 US Banks Targeted Simultaneously
Iranian state actors disrupted online banking for millions of customers at JPMorgan, Bank of America, Wells Fargo, and dozens of other institutions.
Your Preparation
Financial Resilience
A one-week cash reserve in small bills, financial documentation, and a clear plan for when every card terminal and ATM is offline.
The Threat
February 2024 — Change Healthcare
Prescription Dispensing Nationwide Disrupted
A single cyberattack disrupted pharmacy systems across the US for weeks. Patients with chronic conditions could not fill life-sustaining prescriptions.
Your Preparation
Health & Medication
A 30-day prescription buffer, insulin storage without refrigeration, and first aid capability when hospital access is limited.
The Threat
2024 — Volt Typhoon

Patricia de Hemricourt

Patricia de Hemricourt spent a decade in product marketing roles at cybersecurity companies including Cymulate, Cybersixgill (acquired by Bisight), and Portshift (acquired by Cisco), watching sophisticated threats inch steadily closer to the infrastructure ordinary families depend on every day.

Living in Israel, where readiness for any contingency is a cultural reflex rather than a choice, transformed that professional knowledge into a personal mission. The Practical Home Preparedness Guide bridges the gap between what the industry knows and what families can actually do about it.

She is also the author of several children's books, including the One Day You'll Be… series, and a grandmother of four.
