About the Author

Patricia de Hemricourt

Patricia de Hemricourt spent a decade in product marketing roles at cybersecurity companies including Cymulate, Cybersixgill (acquired by Bisight), and Portshift (acquired by Cisco), watching sophisticated threats inch steadily closer to the infrastructure ordinary American families depend on every day.

Living in Israel, where readiness for any contingency is a cultural reflex rather than a choice, transformed that professional knowledge into a personal mission. Protect Your Family from Cyberattacks bridges the gap between what the industry knows and what families can actually do about it.

She is also the author of several children's books, including the One Day You'll Be… series, and a grandmother of four.
